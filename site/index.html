Founder & principal

Meet David. An operator who has been on every side of change.

Lafayette is led by David Demri, a technical solutions consultant, founder, and hands-on builder. His career has moved between entrepreneurship, independent consulting, agency leadership, and long-term transformation mandates inside ambitious companies.

He has built and scaled companies, advised established organizations, and embedded with teams for missions lasting from months to years. That range matters: he knows how change looks from the founder’s seat, the consultant’s side of the table, and inside the organization expected to make it stick.

Extensive travel, international work, and years of cross-cultural collaboration have made David comfortable wherever the work happens. He brings the calm, adaptability, and scar tissue of a seasoned field operator—close enough to solve the problem, experienced enough to see around corners.
